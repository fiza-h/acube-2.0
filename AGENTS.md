# AGENTS.md

## Content Rules

- For landing pages, marketing pages, and pricing pages, optimize for low cognitive load first.
- Use plain English. Prefer everyday words over internal jargon, clever phrases, or abstract branding terms.
- Lead with the answer or outcome. Users should understand the point from the heading and first sentence.
- Keep one idea per sentence and one job per section.
- Prefer short sentences and short paragraphs.
- If a line does not help the user decide, trust, or act, cut it.
- Make actions visible. Keep one clear primary CTA and one clear secondary CTA.
- Prefer recognition over recall: show labels, choices, and next steps directly instead of implying them.
- Keep lists short. Prefer 3 items when possible.
- If technical terms are necessary, explain them in plain language the first time.
- Write for scanning: clear headings, short subheads, concise bullets, and strong first lines.
- Avoid repeating the same idea in headline, subhead, cards, and CTA unless repetition is necessary for clarity.

## Voice

- Write for founders and non-technical readers first.
- Sound clear, calm, and direct.
- Do not use marketese, mythology, or inflated claims.
- Do not make the user work to decode the message.

## Page Checks

- The hero should answer: what is this, who is it for, and what should I do next.
- Each section should answer one buyer question only.
- Reduce choices and text before adding more UI.
- When in doubt, shorten the copy and simplify the layout.
